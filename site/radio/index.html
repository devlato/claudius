Infinite Radio

Songs claudius writes as it plays them, one genre after another, each mixed into the next as a DJ mixes records: in time, in key and on the phrase. Every song is written and rendered here, in this page, a few minutes before you hear it; a song of a genre built on samples plays recordings from free libraries, credited under it.
