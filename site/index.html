A beatmaker that listens to its own songs.

claudius picks sounds, writes the parts with music theory, arranges them into a song, mixes and masters it, then listens to the result with a critic and revises it. Every sound is real audio, synthesized in code or recorded from sampled instruments. There is no neural audio generation and no General MIDI.

How Golden Pulse was made

One command. It generated four deep house songs, then bred them for four generations of eight children each. Every child was auditioned on its build-up and first drop, and the four the critic liked best became the next parents. --no-library keeps Apple Loops and your own samples out; the sample packs still play.

02How it works

Compose, render, listen, revise

A song goes round this loop until the critic runs out of complaints or you stop it. Under each stage is what claudius printed while it made Paper Lanterns.

  1. 1Compose

    Music theory writes a blueprint, the song’s genome: tempo, key, chord progressions as Roman numerals, the form, drum patterns, melodic parts with a writing style, every sound and the mix. Scripts, mutations and the studio all edit the same blueprint.

    Paper Lanterns — lofi at 82 BPM in D minor, 56 bars (2:44)
  progression A: i9 iv9 VImaj7 V7
  form: intro 4 | groove 8 | groove2 16 | break 8 | groove3 16 | outro 4

  2. 2Render

    The blueprint is arranged like a DAW session, with fills at phrase ends, builds with a riser and a highpass sweep, and a beat of silence before the drop. Each track is balanced to its role’s loudness, ducked under the kick and sent to reverb and delay; the master lands on the genre’s loudness target.

    master: -12.5 LUFS, true peak -1.6 dBTP (pre-master -9.8 LUFS); done in 32.1 s

  3. 3Listen

    The critic meters the master (loudness, tonal balance per band, stereo width, mono low end), checks the energy arc and the score (melody in key, chord tones on strong beats, bass on the roots), and listens with earshot, a CLAP audio model ported to Rust.

    ears: 'lo-fi hip hop beat' z +3.8 (closest confusion 'hip hop beat with heavy bass' +3.0)
verdict: 94/100 (arc 0.91, genre 0.95, mix 1.00, production 0.76, theory 1.00)

  4. 4Revise

    Every issue names what to change. You change it in the studio or the script, an assistant changes it over MCP, or claudius evolve mutates the song and keeps the children the critic prefers.

    Fix first: no air above 8 kHz: hats and cymbals too quiet or too dark (air -35.4 dB, healthy from -30)

03Script

The song is a script

A claudius script is Rhai with mini-notation patterns borrowed from Strudel. One cycle of a pattern is one bar. This is all of Paper Lanterns:

// lofi in D minor at 82 BPM: a Rhodes, an upright bass, a vibraphone and strings over a recorded kit
blank("lofi"); title("Paper Lanterns"); tempo(82); key("D minor"); swing(0.6); humanize(0.35);
prog("A", "i9 iv9 VImaj7 V7"); bars_per_chord(1);

track("kick",  s("bd ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ bd ~ ~ ~ bd ~ ~ ~ ~ ~").bank("uzu"));
track("snare", s("~ sd ~ sd").bank("acoustic").gain(0.8));
track("hats",  s("hh*8").bank("acoustic").gain("0.9 0.55").degradeBy(0.1));
track("tamb",  s("~ tambourine ~ tambourine").gain(0.5).pan(0.3));
track("keys",  chord("prog").struct("x ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ x ~").voicing(53, 74).s("rhodes").room(0.3));
track("pad",   chord("prog").voicing(57, 72).s("string_ensemble").level(-8));
track("bass",  note("<[d2 ~ ~ a1 ~ ~ d2 ~] [g1 ~ ~ d2 ~ ~ g1 ~] [bb1 ~ ~ f2 ~ ~ bb1 ~] [a1 ~ ~ e2 ~ ~ a1 ~]>")
                 .s("upright_bass"));

let tune = "<[3 ~ 4 3 1 ~ 0 ~] [~ ~ 4 5 2 ~ 1 ~] [5 ~ 6 5 3 ~ 2 ~] [3 ~ ~ ~ 2 ~ ~ ~] "
         + "[3 ~ 4 3 1 ~ 0 ~] [~ ~ 4 5 2 ~ 1 ~] [6 ~ 7 6 5 ~ 3 ~] [2 ~ 3 ~ ~ ~ ~ ~]>";
track("vibes", n(tune).scale("D:minor:pentatonic").octave(4).s("vibes").delay(0.25));

form([sec("intro", 4, "keys pad"), sec("groove", 8, "kick snare hats keys bass"), sec("groove", 16, "*"),
      sec("break", 8, "keys vibes pad").prog("A"), sec("groove", 16, "*"), sec("outro", 4, "keys pad")]);
s("bd ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ bd ~ ~ ~ bd ~ ~ ~ ~ ~")
One bar cut into sixteen steps, with kicks on steps 1, 7 and 11. ~ is a rest, hh*8 is eight hats in a bar.
.bank("acoustic")
Plays the snare and hats from a recorded kit; .bank("uzu") takes the kick from another one.
prog("A", "i9 iv9 VImaj7 V7")
The progression as Roman numerals, so key() can move it. chord("prog") plays whichever chord the song is on, voice-led between MIDI notes 53 and 74.
n(tune).scale("D:minor:pentatonic")
The vibraphone line as scale degrees. < > plays one group per bar, so the tune is eight bars long.
form([sec("intro", 4, "keys pad"), …])
The sections, their lengths in bars, and which tracks play in each. "*" is every track.

Run it, or read any song

# render it, judge it and write the song folder
claudius script paper-lanterns.rhai
# run a script on a saved song: it changes only what the script names
claudius script tweak.rhai --on out/lofi-paper-lanterns-1
# the whole language
claudius reference

Every song prints as a script: the studio’s script tab shows it, GET /api/script returns it, claudius script writes it to song.rhai beside the render, and --print prints it for a saved song. Running that script on the song changes nothing, so a script is also how you read a song. Golden Pulse came out of evolution, and its script was printed this way.

04Studio

A studio, native and in the browser

claudius studio opens the native window. claudius serve serves the same app, compiled to WebAssembly, at http://127.0.0.1:7878. Both are clients of one session, so the window, a browser tab and an assistant can work on the same song at once. Every change renders and is judged in the background, a few seconds for a full song.

Open the studio demo

The demo runs the studio in your browser with a built-in mock in place of the engine. Every panel works; the audio is a rough preview synthesized in the page. For real renders, run claudius studio or claudius serve.

05Assistants

Make songs with Claude, Cursor or ChatGPT

claudius is an MCP server. An assistant connected to it sees the song you have open, edits it with scripts, mutations, evolution or raw edits, renders and judges it, and you hear every change in the studio as it happens.

Claude Code

claude mcp add claudius -- claudius mcp

Claude Desktop

Add this to ~/Library/Application Support/Claude/claude_desktop_config.json and restart Claude. Use the full path that which claudius prints.

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "claudius": {
      "command": "/Users/you/.cargo/bin/claudius",
      "args": ["mcp"]
    }
  }
}

Cursor

The same command and arguments in .cursor/mcp.json:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "claudius": { "command": "claudius", "args": ["mcp"] }
  }
}

claudius mcp drives the studio when one is running on the port, so you and the assistant share the song. When none is running, it starts a session of its own and serves the web studio on the port, so you can still open it.

Clients that speak Streamable HTTP can use http://127.0.0.1:7878/mcp while the studio or claudius serve runs.

ChatGPT

Assistants in the cloud need a public HTTPS address. Run claudius with a token behind a tunnel:

TOKEN=$(openssl rand -hex 16)
echo "$TOKEN"
claudius serve --public --token "$TOKEN"
cloudflared tunnel --url http://127.0.0.1:7878

Then add a custom connector in ChatGPT’s developer mode with the URL https://<tunnel>/mcp?token=<token>. The studio is at https://<tunnel>/?token=<token>.

06Sounds

Real sounds, with their licences

Notes exist only as the score that plays the sounds, like a piano roll. The sounds themselves are synthesized in code or recorded.

Synthesized

13 drum voices; a polyphonic and monophonic synth engine with polyBLEP oscillators, unison with analog drift, SVF and ladder filters, FM and glide, and 20 presets; risers, downlifters, impacts and reverse cymbals. claudius sounds renders every one of them to WAV.

Recorded

14 sample packs with 162 instruments, multi-sampled with velocity layers and round robins, all under licences that allow making and publishing music with them. claudius-packs install --defaults downloads them, about 1.1 GB. Songs that use CC BY packs get a credits.txt.

Apple Loops

When GarageBand or Logic content is installed, claudius uses it too, about 14,000 loops on a full install. Apple licenses these loops for your own music; claudius reads them where GarageBand put them and copies nothing except one-shot cuts in its cache. The songs on this page use none.

Your own sounds

claudius chop cuts any recording into bar loops and drum one-shots for your library, make --sample flips a chop into a song, and scripts play any file with .s("/path/to/sound.wav").

07Install

Install

The install scripts download the latest release, check it against the release’s SHA256SUMS, and put claudius, claudius-packs and the web studio in your user folder. Then they offer to fetch the sample library and the listening models.

macOS and Linux

$ curl -fsSL {{repo_url}}/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Apple silicon and Intel Macs (Intel builds have no ears: their verdicts come from the meters and the theory checks); x86_64 and ARM64 Linux with glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 39 and later). Without ALSA you get the headless build, which serves the studio to your browser. The commands go to ~/.local/bin.

Windows preview

PS> irm {{repo_url}}/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex

In PowerShell. An x64 build, which also runs on ARM64 Windows; it is newer and less tested than the others, so please report what breaks. The commands go to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\claudius\bin, which the script adds to your PATH.

From source

With a Rust toolchain, in a clone of the repository:

cargo install --path crates/claudius-cli       # the claudius command
cargo install --path crates/claudius-packs     # the claudius-packs command
(cd crates/claudius-ui && NO_COLOR=true trunk build --release)   # the web studio (optional; needs trunk)

Then

claudius setup                  # the sample packs (about 1.1 GB) and the listening models (about 0.8 GB)
claudius doctor                 # checks the installation: studio, packs, models, ffmpeg, the port
claudius make --genre house     # a full song in out/house-<title>-<seed>/
claudius studio                 # the studio window, with the engine inside

Without the models, --no-ears judges with meters and theory only. Once they are downloaded, they load in about 4 seconds.
